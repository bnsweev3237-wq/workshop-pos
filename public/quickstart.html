Welcome to your shop

The fastest path from an empty shop to your first real sale — about fifteen minutes, start to finish.

1
Settings → Branding

Make it yours

Add your business name, upload a logo, and pick an accent color. This shows up in the app header, your sign-in screen, and on every printed packing list and price tag.

2
Calculator, or Items

Add your first items

If you make what you sell, use the Calculator: enter your materials, labor time, and a markup or margin, and it works out your price for you. If you buy things ready-made to resell, add them straight from the Items tab with a cost and sale price. Either way, saving an item there is what makes it sellable.

3
Settings → Users

Bring in your team

Add an account for anyone else ringing up sales. Cashier accounts are locked to the Sell page only — no pricing, no reports. Skip this entirely if it's just you.

4
Sell

Ring up your first sale

Tap an item, tender it by cash or card, and you're done. Add a customer's email at checkout to send a receipt and offer them your mailing list. The Sell page works offline too — a sale made with no connection queues up and syncs once you're back online.

5
Reports

Know your numbers

Revenue, profit, and cost broken down by item and by who rang it up. Running a market or fair? Set it up as an Event first (Sell page) and Reports shows its "true profit" after booth cost, card fees, and anything else it took to be there.

Worth knowing about, when you need it

Custom Orders
Track a made-to-order piece from request to pickup, separate from your regular catalog.
Consignment
Inventory placed with a shop or gallery — what's out, what sold, what's owed back after their commission.
Automatic backups
A full backup is taken daily with no setup needed from you.
Email alerts
Turn on a low-stock digest in Settings so you hear about it before you're out.
